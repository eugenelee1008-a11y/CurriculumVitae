Research in progress

Dissertation · Chapter 1 · Working title

NLP-Based Analysis of Public Attitudes Toward Climate Change in Korea

Analyzes approximately 50,000 Korean-language YouTube comments using LDA topic modeling and a fine-tuned KoELECTRA sentiment classifier. The project studies public attitudes toward climate-related issues while emphasizing annotation quality, context, and model validation.

Dissertation · Chapter 2 · Working title

Evaluating California’s Cap-and-Trade Program Using Facility-Level Emissions

Empirical analysis of emissions and regulatory coverage using facility-level data, with attention to changes in coverage and credible treatment-effect identification.

Dissertation · Chapter 3 · Working title

Difference-in-Differences with Changing Treatment Qualification

Develops stayer-based difference-in-differences and conditional response function approaches for time-varying treatment qualification. Uses simulations and an application to California facility emissions to investigate identification assumptions and treatment-effect heterogeneity.
