Job Market Paper — Chapter 2

Difference-in-Differences with Changing Qualification: Theory, Simulation, and Evidence from U.S. NOx Regulation

Estimates how successive U.S. NOx policies affect ozone-season emissions when qualification changes across the policies, using EPA regulatory and emissions data, simulations, and a Difference-in-differences (DD) framework with time-varying qualification.

Other Dissertation Research

Chapter 1 — NLP-Based Analysis of Public Attitudes Toward Climate Change in Korea

Analyzes approximately 50,000 Korean YouTube comments using Latent Dirichlet Allocation topic modeling and a fine-tuned KoELECTRA sentiment classifier (transformer model; deep-learning architecture for NLP), with manually labeled data and model-validation procedures, to find differences in sentiments across categories of climate-related videos.

Chapter 3 — Difference-in-Differences with Varying Qualification: Untreated Moving Effects and Past-Dependent Treatment Effects

Uses "stayer-based DD" for analyzing treatment effects under settings with time-varying qualification/eligibility. Uses California facility-level emissions data to investigate Cap-and-Trade policy effects on emissions of greenhouse gases and local pollutants, such as Nitrogen Oxides, while accounting for untreated outcome changes.
